2024

The Supernatural Power Troll · puzzle platformer · GameDev.js Jam → Steam

  • Fifty levels on one readable sentence: one key, one door, one exit; fruit is the only safe way to touch a human.
  • NPCs assembled from drawn parts with generated, spoken, always-subtitled dialogue, shown only on events.
  • In-game level editor that exposes only platforms, items and humans; community levels one click from the menu. 63 Aseprite sources, music and sound, all mine; listed on Kotaku.
2011 → 2023

World of Turtle · Sokoban puzzle · BASIC → Flash → TypeScript / WebGL on Steam and Google Play

  • Same rules rebuilt three times; the interface changed every time: ASCII to sprites, mouse to touch and gamepad.
  • Ten stages of ten levels on one screen; every new element taught in the world, no tutorial screens; skip passes instead of lives.
  • English, French and Korean with full audio; one-column menus sized for thumb, stick and mouse.
2014

Blink Pong · Darwin Gator · accessibility games · LowRezJam, AccessibilityJam · App Store, Google Play

  • A pong played with eyes closed (the ball is where the sound is) and a full platformer on a single button.
  • Vision, cognition and motor load each reduced to one thing; sound and timing designed before pixels.
