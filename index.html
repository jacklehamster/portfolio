About

I write songs the way I make games: one idea, one language, one cover, then I ship it and start the next one. The songs come out in English, French, Korean or Vietnamese depending on who they are for. Some of them are about trolls.

BUG is the first screenplay I let other people read. It reached the finals of Cinema Royale in Paris in 2026, which I am still getting used to.

By day I build the web cockpit that flies drones at Skydio. By night, this.
